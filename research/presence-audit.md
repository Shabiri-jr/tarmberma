# Tamberma Ibadan: public digital presence audit

Research date: 30 September 2026. Read-only inspection; no enquiries, bookings, orders, or publications were made. This file records the digital-presence workstream; the main research brief should incorporate the separately inspected Instagram profile and supplied PDFs.

## Most useful findings

### Current primary funnel: Instagram to Linktree to menus / WhatsApp

- The root researcher observed the Instagram bio linking to [linktr.ee/tamberma](https://linktr.ee/tamberma). Independently opening that page confirms the name **Tamberma Ibadan** and a positioning around family dining, comfort food, and warm hospitality.
- The public Linktree offers two PDF cards, Food Menu and Drinks Menu, plus Instagram and WhatsApp social links. It does not display a table-booking form, event calendar, opening hours, address, or delivery card in the inspected content.
- The root researcher's separate live Instagram inspection confirmed two additional bio destinations: a direct Glovo link (store ID 573085) and an [October pop-up vendor form](https://forms.gle/NZaJ4gvhurEg87Ky7). Therefore delivery is already linked from Instagram; its omission from Linktree must not be portrayed as absence from the whole social funnel.
- Its page HTML identifies the WhatsApp destination as [2348075590939](https://api.whatsapp.com/send?phone=2348075590939) and links Instagram to [@tamberma_ibadan](https://instagram.com/tamberma_ibadan).
- **Both supplied PDF filenames exactly match the documents linked from this page**, establishing that the supplied files correspond to the public Instagram-bio menu links. Food filename is dated 1 August 2026; drinks filename has no revision date. This confirms provenance, not future price validity.

Primary PDF URLs:

- [Food menu](https://ugc.production.linktr.ee/1ecd8a82-67fd-4c98-8f87-054992f046a1_Tamberma-Ibd-Food-Menu---1-Aug-2026.pdf)
- [Drinks menu](https://ugc.production.linktr.ee/53108378-6552-4bb3-97d5-e3b6c5b258d3_Tamberma-Ibd-Drinks-Menu.pdf)

Source quality: primary channel linked by the current Instagram profile; page text and raw HTML inspected. Current operating hours and actual WhatsApp responsiveness were not tested.

### Delivery already exists on Glovo

[Glovo's Tamberma Ibadan store](https://glovoapp.com/en/ng/ringroad-ibadan-challenge/stores/tamberma-ibadan-rch) resolves and displays the merchant name, RingRoad Ibadan/Challenge service area, and a large browsable food menu. It includes Indian, Chinese, Nigerian, BBQ, continental, pasta, desserts, and pizza categories. The page asks visitors for a delivery address.

Source quality: primary marketplace storefront, opened successfully; the root researcher additionally confirmed a direct Glovo destination in Instagram's bio links. No checkout was attempted. Do not represent its delivery prices as dine-in prices; availability, charges, and eligibility depend on the address and time. A website should send orders to this established channel rather than assuming a new ordering backend is required.

### A discoverable legacy Ibadan landing page exists

[tamberma.netlify.app](https://tamberma.netlify.app/) is live. [Snapchat's Ibadan place listing](https://www.snapchat.com/place/tamberma-ibadan/66fb7832-4f39-11f0-ae8c-833c9e451589) links to it as the website, but official current ownership/maintenance is **unconfirmed**.

Observed on that site:

- Footer: **Adaego © 2021**; address **894, Near EFCC, Iyaganku GRA**; phone **+2348054090607**; email **tambermang@gmail.com**.
- Only four food stories appear: tandoori chicken, chicken suya pizza, grilled prawns, Singapore noodles. No full priced menu or PDF download was found.
- Source inspection confirms **Book Now** is an anchor to the footer phone text (`#phone`), not a reservation form. The phone is plain text, with no `tel:` link.
- About and Contact links are working in-page anchors. Facebook/Twitter icons point to `#`; Instagram points to the older `tamberma_ng` handle.
- The old address/phone differ from the current profile and Linktree. These are inconsistent public records, not proof of an exact relocation date.

Source quality: direct site inspection proves its contents; a third-party listing proves discoverability. It does not prove the restaurant currently controls the site. Pitch it as an opportunity to consolidate public information, not as a proven failure by management.

### Do not confuse Ibadan with Lagos / Victoria Island

[tambermaventures.com](https://www.tambermaventures.com/) presents Tamberma Ventures Limited in Lagos. Its footer names **23 Saka Tinubu Street, Victoria Island**, **tamberma.vi@gmail.com**, and **+2348054090607**. Its [reservation page](https://www.tambermaventures.com/reservations) exposes party-size, date, and time controls. Nothing inspected establishes that this form books Ibadan.

[linktr.ee/tambermavi](https://linktr.ee/tambermavi) is explicitly Tamberma VI / Lagos. It has food and drinks PDF cards and an Order on Glovo link. These are different branch links. The reused 08054090607 number makes branch labelling particularly important.

Source quality: direct site contents; relationship of legal entity and branch administration is unverified. Do not copy VI services, prices, reservations, or contact details into the Ibadan prototype.

## Pitch opportunities supported by those observations

| Observed friction | Website opportunity | Evidence boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Menus reached through separate PDF cards | Searchable HTML menu with cuisine filters, readable mobile prices, and original PDFs as downloads | Do not claim PDFs are unusable; this is a usability improvement hypothesis |
| Manual WhatsApp contact route | Persistent Reserve / WhatsApp actions with date, party size, and occasion included in a draft enquiry | A request is not a confirmed reservation; restaurant workflow must define confirmation |
| Delivery merchant exists but is absent from inspected Ibadan Linktree | Clearly labelled Order on Glovo button | Verify business-approved destination and delivery coverage before launch |
| Different public addresses/phones and multiple branches | One prominent Ibadan location block, directions, verified hours, and branch-specific contact information | Current hours need owner confirmation; do not blindly copy directories |
| Instagram has event-oriented highlights (root observation) | Manageable upcoming-events section with expiry dates and event enquiry path | Historical events do not prove a current recurring schedule |
| Legacy site shows little of the full experience | Short, optional 3D atmosphere sequence feeding into menu, visit, and booking actions | No measured conversion loss or uplift is known |

Suggested core journeys: **Explore the atmosphere → view food / drinks → request a table**; **check a dish → order on Glovo**; **browse upcoming event → enquire / book through the approved channel**; **find hours/address → open directions**.

Current primary event evidence from the root's live Instagram inspection: [October pop-up post](https://www.instagram.com/tamberma_ibadan/p/DdpNjMtMBzi/) advertises Saturday 10 and Sunday 11 October, noon to 9 pm; the image identifies 2026. The [22 September entertainment carousel](https://www.instagram.com/tamberma_ibadan/p/Ddmaq01DHan/) promotes weekly live band, karaoke, magician, and saxophonist programming; a visible slide lists FM GURU on Sunday, 7–10 pm. Confirm future recurrence with the business before turning these into permanent website promises.

## Confirmation list before a public build

Obtain the business's current address wording and map pin, hours, official phone/email, booking confirmation process, Glovo link approval, upcoming-event schedule, menu revision owner, website/domain ownership, and rights to supplied photos/video. These are production dependencies; they do not block a clearly labelled private concept pitch.

Do not claim that Tamberma has no website, no online ordering, or no reservation channel. The evidence supports a stronger, narrower proposition: **unify a fragmented public journey around the atmosphere and make the next action easy**.
