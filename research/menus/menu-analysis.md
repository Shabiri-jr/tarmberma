## Dietary and service implications

- There are explicitly named vegetarian menu areas: Indian veg appetizers/curries and paneer, Chinese vegetarian noodles/rice/starters, vegetarian grill items and two pizzas carrying green markers. This supports a 'vegetarian options' claim, not a blanket vegan/gluten-free/halal claim.
- No formal allergen key or dietary policy is provided. Descriptions mention milk, cottage cheese, yogurt, cashew, shellfish, wheat-based breads, eggs/egg white and other possible allergens. Verify with the kitchen before using allergy-safe tags or claims.
- Chinese dishes use medium/large sizes; chicken grills use half/full; pizzas use medium/large and specialty formats. A data model needs variants and clear unit labels.
- Nigerian soup, swallow and protein are separately priced except the explicitly bundled special soups. Avoid automatically bundling them in an online order total.
- Continental soups explicitly come with garlic bread (food p8).
- The menus do not establish delivery, takeaway, reservation capability, private events, live entertainment, opening hours or booking availability.

## Data cleanup / owner questions before production

1. Verify effective dates, currency, all current prices and VAT display. The drinks file metadata is from 2025 and the food filename/metadata dates differ.
2. Confirm serving units for house wine, spirits and premium bottles. Some spirits show two unlabeled prices, e.g. Sierra Blanco 3,500 / 50,000 and Bombay Sapphire 3,500 / 50,000. These may be measures/bottles but the menu does not label them.
3. White Wine repeats 'Villa Antinori Toscana Rosso - 100,000', also listed under Red Wine. Preserve as a source anomaly; ask the owner for the intended white wine before publishing.
4. The 'Single Malt Whiskey' section contains several types of whisky. Revise public taxonomy with owner approval.
5. Confirm spellings, recipes and undefined platter composition/serving count. Do not extrapolate from names.
6. Some raw PDF text is incomplete or duplicated: drinks text layers repeat content 3 times; drinks p2 names/prices are absent; food p7 triple-decker prices and food p9 grilled-chicken pineapple-rice label are missing in extraction. This memo uses visual confirmation to correct those gaps.
7. Food p1's Hot & Sour soup description reads 'A creamy Tomato Soup', a likely source copy error. Ask the kitchen before reproducing.

## Implications for a 3D website pitch

- A short, optional 3D moment could spotlight the setting or a signature platter/cocktail. The PDFs alone cannot support a faithful model of the restaurant; obtain owner-authorized venue references first.
- Keep menu browsing in fast, searchable HTML with cuisine filters and clear size variants, plus downloadable PDFs. 3D should not be required to read a price or start a booking enquiry.
- Potential content anchors grounded in menu evidence: 'Six food worlds, one destination' (six major food headings), Indian specialties, Nigerian favorites, grill house, signature cocktails and sharing platters. This is proposed marketing language, not an existing slogan.
- Strong crossover detail: chicken/beef/shrimp suya pizzas and chicken-tikka/butter-chicken pizzas connect Nigerian, Indian and pizza choices; this is more distinctive than generic luxury-restaurant copy.
- Do not describe signature items as bestsellers unless the business confirms sales/customer preference data.
