## Recommendation

Build the pitch around **“Step into Tamberma”**: a warm, garden-inspired digital experience that leads visitors to food, drinks, events, directions and a table enquiry. A short 3D scene can communicate the atmosphere; the useful information should load immediately and remain easy to reach.

The evidence points to a restaurant with an active social presence, a substantial menu and an events programme. Its customer journey is spread across Instagram, Linktree, PDFs, WhatsApp, Glovo and an event registration form. The commercial proposition is to connect those existing channels and express the venue's personality more clearly. There is no evidence here to quantify lost bookings or promise a particular increase in revenue.

## 2. Brand story and visual identity

The most useful brand ingredients are **food, greenery, warm materials, sociability and a connection to craft**.

A public [Zeperoni listing](https://www.zeperoni.com/listing/tamberma-ibadan/) attributes the name and concept to the culture and architecture of northern Togo and says local artisans made furniture and décor. Treat these as a reported brand story for the owner to confirm, not as freshly verified operating claims.

For accurate cultural context, UNESCO identifies [Koutammakou, the Land of the Batammariba](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1140/) as a living cultural landscape in Togo and Benin, known for earthen tower-houses. That heritage designation concerns the landscape, not the restaurant. Any website story should make the distinction clear and avoid inventing an endorsement or an architectural replica.

Observed visual cues:

- Instagram's logo/highlight treatment uses black, orange and light lettering, with a roof-like mark above a steaming bowl.
- Current event graphics use burnt orange, cream, brown and expressive display lettering.
- The drinks PDF uses a peach, mottled background, orange type and line-art glassware.
- The food PDF uses a pale botanical background, bright geometric borders, orange script section labels and cuisine-related illustrations.
- Historical venue photography and guest descriptions point toward outdoor greenery and rustic materials. A site visit or current owner-supplied imagery is needed to model the venue accurately.

**Proposed design palette, not official colour specifications:** clay orange `#B9572E`, warm cream `#F3E9DC`, charcoal `#211B17`, dark leaf green `#354738`. Use generous photographic areas, readable menu typography and restrained texture. Ask for the original logo files before final production.

These are **listed menu prices, exclusive of the 7.5% VAT noted in the documents**. The PDFs do not visibly print a currency symbol or code; the naira labels above are a contextual interpretation for this Nigerian restaurant. They are not a quotation from the restaurant today. The food filename says 1 August 2026, but its PDF metadata predates that; the drinks file has no visible revision date in its filename. File metadata is not proof of menu validity. The restaurant should sign off the publication version.

The food menu covers Indian, Chinese, grills, Nigerian, pizza and continental dishes. The drinks menu includes cold beverages, beer, milkshakes, shots, fresh juice, mocktails, cocktails and spirits/wine categories. Cuisine filters should be supplemented with plain-language categories such as starters, mains, sides and drinks.

A practical menu model needs item name, description, category, size/portion variants, price, availability and an update date. Some Chinese dishes and pizzas have size-dependent prices. A dish such as jollof may appear as a meal and as an extra; these must remain distinct items. Vegetarian sections support discovery, but ingredient names alone do not establish vegan, allergy-safe or halal status. Staff need to verify any such tags.

The menu review also found content to clarify before transcription: a repeated red-wine-labelled product in the white-wine section, mixed whisky classifications and unclear units for some paired spirit prices. The PDFs state no separate service fee. Do not infer that none can apply; ask for the complete publication policy. Raw extraction contains duplicated or missing text layers, so use the visually checked analysis when building the menu data.

Use real food photography for appetite and portion expectations. Model one distinctive object well for the pitch rather than attempting to manufacture accurate 3D versions of every dish. A named house cocktail is a stronger initial 3D candidate than a generic floating plate.

## 5. Recommended 3D concept: “Step into Tamberma”

**Creative proposition:** a welcoming digital arrival, moving from a garden atmosphere to a table, then to the evening programme.

| Scene | Proposed visual / interaction | Visitor's useful next action |
|---|---|---|
| Arrival | A short depth effect through foliage toward a warmly lit table; immediate headline and navigation | View menus / request a table |
| At the table | A few venue-inspired materials and objects, with real photography of selected dishes | Browse cuisine and prices |
| At the bar | One carefully modelled house drink, with a small optional rotation | Explore drinks |
| Stay for the evening | The scene warms toward evening; current event cards appear in normal page content | View the programme / make an enquiry |
| Plan your visit | Clear Ibadan address, verified hours, contact and map link | Get directions / call / WhatsApp |

This is an art-directed concept, not a surveyed digital twin. Any exact floor plan, table layout or capacity would require venue access and measurement.

Suggested draft hero copy:

> **Good food. Warm company. A little escape.**
>
> Discover Tamberma in Iyaganku, Ibadan. Explore the menu, find your next evening out, and plan your visit.

Suggested navigation: **Experience · Food & Drinks · What's On · Our Story · Visit**. Use **Request a table** as the primary action while the booking process is manual. A submitted enquiry must not be presented as a confirmed reservation.

For mobile, open with a strong poster image and the same useful controls. Load the optional 3D enhancement after core content. Provide reduced motion, keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus states and a static fallback. Scrolling should remain normal; menus and booking actions must never depend on traversing the scene.

## 6. Build scope and technical direction

**Pitch prototype:** one polished responsive landing page, one short 3D sequence, a small verified menu sample, events, visit information and a clearly labelled enquiry demonstration. Include desktop and mobile examples plus a short recorded walkthrough. A private concept can use labelled placeholders where permission or assets are still pending.

**First production release:** owner-approved menu and photography, editable events/menu content, confirmed contact and map information, working enquiry delivery, approved Glovo route, accessible fallback, search metadata and measurement. No need to introduce payment or live inventory unless the restaurant actually wants to operate those systems.

**Later options:** a photographed 360° venue tour, a reservation provider integration, private-event enquiries and richer venue exploration, if demand and operations justify them.

An appropriate implementation is a fast HTML-first React site with an isolated Three.js / React Three Fiber scene. Use compressed glTF assets, baked lighting and limited materials; pause or reduce animation when not visible. Three.js provides [glTF loading and compression integration](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/GLTFLoader.html), and the [React Three Fiber performance guide](https://r3f.docs.pmnd.rs/next/advanced/scaling-performance) covers on-demand rendering. Final framework selection depends on the team's editing and hosting needs.

Set project budgets during prototyping: aim for a small initial scene download, avoid large texture sets and test actual mid-range phones and slower mobile connections. These are proposed acceptance conditions, not measured results. Target good Core Web Vitals at the 75th percentile: LCP ≤2.5 seconds, INP ≤200 ms and CLS ≤0.1, following [Google's guidance](https://web.dev/articles/vitals).

Useful success measures are menu use, table-enquiry submissions, WhatsApp clicks, directions clicks, outbound Glovo clicks and event enquiries. A WhatsApp or Glovo click is intent, not a confirmed booking or completed order. Confirmed outcomes require a matching operational process; do not infer revenue from click counts.

## 8. How to pitch it

Lead with the customer's decision: **“What will it feel like to spend an evening here, and how do I plan it?”** Then demonstrate how the proposed site answers that question in a few clear actions.

Suggested spoken pitch:

> “Tamberma already has a rich story: food from several cuisines, drinks, entertainment and a community market. I want to bring that experience together online. Guests would get a short, immersive introduction to the atmosphere, then move straight to menus, upcoming events, directions or a table enquiry. The site would connect to your existing WhatsApp and Glovo channels, and we would measure the enquiries and actions it generates.”

A useful pitch presentation contains: the observed customer journey, the proposed experience, a 60–90 second walkthrough, the mobile version, the operational requirements, and a clear prototype-to-launch scope. Show the owner's actual dishes, brand cues and event programme so that the proposal feels specific to Tamberma.

Avoid unsupported claims such as “you have no website,” “you have no delivery,” “this will double reservations,” or “Ibadan's first 3D restaurant.” Do not promise a booking engine when the first release only prepares an enquiry.

## 9. Owner discovery and asset requirements

Before production, establish:

1. Who approves the project and who controls existing domains and social links?
2. Which goal matters most: table enquiries, group occasions, delivery, event attendance or vendor applications?
3. What address, map pin, phone, email, hours and kitchen closing times should be published?
4. How are table requests accepted, confirmed, changed or cancelled? Are deposits involved?
5. Which menu revision is approved, what fees should be displayed, and who will update prices and availability?
6. Which events recur, who maintains them, and what happens after a vendor application or event enquiry?
7. Are there approved group packages and genuinely available spaces to show?
8. Can the team supply the vector logo, current venue photos, food/drink photography and permission to use them?

For the visual work, request daylight and evening coverage of the entrance, garden, indoor seating and bar; close-ups of distinctive furniture, pottery, textures and signage; clean photographs of a small chef-selected dish set; and several views of the chosen cocktail. A full venue model requires a more detailed capture plan. Use real images or explicitly labelled concept visuals so guests are not shown invented facilities.
